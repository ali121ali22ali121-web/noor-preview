The Best Results Look Like You on a Better Day

At Noor, facial aesthetic consultations are quiet, considered, and medically led. Dr. Fatima will assess your concerns, facial movement, medical history, and suitability before any treatment pathway is discussed.

The aim is never to change who you are. It is to provide honest guidance, realistic expectations, and a plan that respects your features.

Common Areas of Concern

Forehead Lines

Horizontal expression lines that can appear when raising the eyebrows.

Frown Lines

Vertical lines between the brows, often associated with repeated expression.

Crow's Feet

Fine lines around the outer eye area that can develop with smiling and movement.

Aftercare Principles

Aftercare depends on the treatment plan chosen following consultation. If a treatment is performed, Dr. Fatima will provide clear, written aftercare instructions before you leave the clinic.

  • Follow clinician-specific aftercare
  • Avoid heat and strenuous exercise if advised
  • Do not massage treated areas unless instructed
  • Contact the clinic with any concerns
