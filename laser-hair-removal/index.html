Your First Visit

01

Consultation & Patch Test

Every patient begins with a private consultation and patch test. Dr. Fatima assesses skin type, hair type, medical history, and goals.

02

Preparation

Shave the treatment area 24 hours before. Avoid tanning, waxing, and plucking before treatment.

03

Treatment

Most patients describe treatment as a quick warm snap. Timings vary by area, from minutes to longer full-body sessions.

04

Aftercare

Avoid sun exposure, hot showers, saunas, and intense exercise for the advised period after treatment.

Most patients require a course of 6 sessions for optimal results, spaced according to the area being treated. Hair grows in cycles, and consistent treatment helps target hair during the active growth phase.

PREPARATION & AFTERCARE

Before and After Your Appointment

Before Treatment

  • Shave the treatment area 24 hours before
  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning
  • No waxing or plucking for 4 weeks
  • Arrive with clean skin

After Treatment

  • Use SPF 50 on exposed areas
  • Avoid heat and intense exercise for 24-48 hours
  • Apply soothing aftercare if advised
  • Shaving only between sessions

Optional in-clinic shaving is available for £10.
